Cebio — Delete Your Account & Data

App: Cebio · Developer: [PROVIDER NAME / COMPANY]

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Cebio and sign in.
  2. Tap the person icon (top right) → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and all my data and confirm.

The Delete my account option in the account menu does the same.

If the app is not installed

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address registered to your account with the subject "Cebio account deletion request". After verifying your identity we delete your account within 30 days (usually within a few days) and confirm by email.

Request deletion

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, password hash, preferences and consent records)
  • All receipts and items, bank/card transactions and import records
  • Subscriptions, price history, budgets, usage counters and reported AI answers

Deletion is immediate and permanent and cannot be undone. Backup copies are erased within [BACKUP RETENTION, e.g. 30] days. Nothing is kept in a "deactivated" state.

What is not deleted

If you have Premium, your store subscription is not cancelled automatically; cancel it in your App Store / Google Play account settings. Purchase records are kept by Apple / Google under their own policies.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual receipts, transactions, subscriptions or budgets in the app, undo a whole statement import, and turn off AI data sharing in Settings.
