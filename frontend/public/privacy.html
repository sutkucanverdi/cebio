3. Purposes & Legal Bases

  • Creating your account and providing the spending, subscription, budget and report features — performance of a contract
  • AI analysis of receipts, statements and questions, including transfer abroad — your separate, optional explicit consent
  • Security, abuse prevention and reviewing reported AI answers — legitimate interests
  • Managing store subscriptions and meeting legal obligations

Your data is never used for advertising or profiling and is never sold.

4. AI Processing & International Transfers

Receipt scanning, statement analysis and the assistant work only if you give explicit consent. Without it, your data is not sent to any AI service, and you can still add receipts manually and use subscriptions, budgets and reports. You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings → Data sharing with AI.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (Gemini API); servers may be located outside your country, including the USA.
  • What is sent: the receipt photo / statement file you upload; your question and the spending summaries needed to answer it. Your email and password are never sent.
  • Purpose: only to read the document or answer the question.
  • Provider use: we use the paid Gemini API; under Google's terms for this service, content is not used to improve Google's products and may be retained only briefly for abuse monitoring.

App data is stored at [SERVER LOCATION / HOSTING PROVIDER]. If in-app purchases are used, subscription status is verified via RevenueCat Inc. (USA).

5. Retention

Your data is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, all of your personal data — receipts, bank transactions, subscriptions, budgets, reports and usage — is deleted immediately and permanently. Backup copies are erased within [BACKUP RETENTION, e.g. 30] days. Store purchase records are kept by Apple / Google under their own policies.

6. Your Rights

You can access, correct, export (CSV/Excel), and delete your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your local supervisory authority. Delete your account and all data in the app via Settings → Delete my account and all my data (see Account & data deletion). For other requests email [CONTACT EMAIL]; we reply free of charge within 30 days.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), passwords are hashed with bcrypt, each user can only access their own data, and changing your password signs out old sessions. The AI assistant has read-only access to your data.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
