FAQ

A receipt wasn't read or an amount is wrong.
Retake the photo flat, in good light, with the whole receipt visible. Fix any misread line with Edit in the receipt list. Prefer no AI? Use Add manually without AI.

Scanning / the assistant asks for "permission".
These features send your data to Google Gemini and work only with your consent. Turn on Settings → Data sharing with AI.

I hit my monthly limit.
The free plan has monthly limits for AI features that reset at the start of each month. See your usage under My plan & usage.

How do I cancel Premium?
iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On a new phone, tap My plan → Restore purchases.

I don't get reminder notifications.
Turn on Reminders on the Subscriptions screen and allow notifications for Cebio in your phone settings.

Is my statement safe?
The statement file is not stored; only the rows you confirm are saved. Card numbers and balances are never stored.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete my account and all my data. Details: Account & data deletion.
